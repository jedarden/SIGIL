## Why This Works

The RAII (Resource Acquisition Is Initialization) pattern ensures:
1. When `DaemonGuard` is created, it takes ownership of the `Child` process
2. When the guard goes out of scope (normal return or panic), `Drop::drop()` is called
3. The `Drop` implementation calls `kill()` to terminate the daemon
4. The `wait()` call reaps the zombie process

This cleanup happens even if the test panics, ensuring no orphaned daemons.
